360° training

Step inside a real workplace and find the threats

Trinova's signature experience is a fully explorable 360° environment. Walk through the workspace, look around and click on the eight hidden threat hotspots. Each hotspot opens a practical scenario with guidance you can apply the same day.

  • 1 Explore the environment freely in any direction
  • 2 Discover 8 hotspots — phishing emails, weak devices, tailgating and more
  • 3 Read the scenario, mark the topic complete and build your progress
  • 4 Finish with the quiz to unlock your certificate
